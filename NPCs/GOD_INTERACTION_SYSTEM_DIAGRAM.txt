╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                         PLAYER EXPERIENCE FLOW                               ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

1. CHARACTER CREATION
   └─> Player selects Athena as their god
       └─> GodManager.selected_god = ATHENA

2. ENTER SAFEZONE
   └─> Player spawns in safezone map
       └─> Sees 8 god sprites around the area

3. APPROACH ATHENA (Player's God)
   └─> Player walks near Athena sprite
       └─> "Press G to talk" appears above Athena
           └─> Player presses G
               └─> Athena: "Greetings, my champion..."
                   └─> Athena: "Your special skill: Aegis Shield"
                       └─> [SKILL UNLOCKED: Aegis Shield]
                           └─> Player can now use Aegis Shield ability!

4. APPROACH ZEUS (Not Player's God)
   └─> Player walks near Zeus sprite
       └─> "Press G to talk" appears above Zeus
           └─> Player presses G
               └─> Zeus: "You serve Athena, not me."
                   └─> Zeus: "Seek your own god for guidance."
                       └─> No skill unlock, just rejection dialogue

╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                         TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK REF                            ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

Problem: "Press G" doesn't appear
└─> Solution: Check Area2D and CollisionShape2D are added correctly

Problem: Nothing happens when pressing G
└─> Solution: Verify "interact" input action exists (already configured)

Problem: Wrong dialogue appears
└─> Solution: Check god_type matches the god (Athena=1, Zeus=2, etc.)

Problem: All gods reject player
└─> Solution: Ensure player selected a god during character creation

Problem: Script errors in console
└─> Solution: Verify GodManager is in autoload list
